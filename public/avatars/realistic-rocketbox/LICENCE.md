All six were chosen by the founder on 18 Nov 2026 from a picture sheet of the library's 38 everyday adults. The page shows them as numbered looks; none is labelled by national or ethnic origin.

The colour pictures are loaded next to the model by `src/components/avatar/HumanStage.jsx`; they are not packed inside the GLB.

## What these characters are, and are not

- Each is a sculpted 3D person with a skeleton, drawn on the device like a game character.
- It is not a photograph or video of a real person, and it is not generated by AI.
- They are stand-ins to show the engine, not Aaria's final look, and whether they are good enough to show users is the founder's decision on real screenshots.

## Made smaller for phone data (20 Nov 2026)

Changed with `scripts/avatar-shrink.py`, which is in this repository. The look was compared
before and after on rendered pictures; no visible difference.

Each `.glb` keeps only the 22 face shapes the app can use - the 15 speech shapes
(`AA_VI_00_Sil` to `AA_VI_14_U`), blink, smile left and right, cheek raise, frown left and
right, and eyes up - and drops the other 153 of 175. Shape, skeleton and those 22 shapes are
untouched. Command:
`python3 scripts/avatar-shrink.py ORIGINAL.glb OUT.glb --keep-morphs <the 22 names>`.
The hair-and-lash pictures (`*_opacity_color.png`) are stored with at most 256 colours,
transparency kept. The sizes and fingerprints in the table further up are of the files
as first converted; these are the files now in the app:
