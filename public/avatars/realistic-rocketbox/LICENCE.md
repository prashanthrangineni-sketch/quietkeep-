## What this character is, and is not

- It is a sculpted 3D person with a skeleton, drawn on the device like a game character.
- It is not a photograph or video of a real person, and it is not generated by AI.
- It is a stand-in to show the engine. It is not Aaria's final look, and whether it is good enough to show users is the founder's decision on real screenshots.
