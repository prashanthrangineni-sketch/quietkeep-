## What these characters are, and are not

- Each is a sculpted 3D person with a skeleton, drawn on the device like a game character.
- It is not a photograph or video of a real person, and it is not generated by AI.
- They are stand-ins to show the engine, not Aaria's final look, and whether they are good enough to show users is the founder's decision on real screenshots.
