## Summary

Implements **AG-01: Overtaking Advisor v1 (DRV-12a)**.
Provides overtaking assistance without a camera by utilizing community hazard spot history and GPS track curvature analysis.

### What Was Built
1. **`src/lib/overtake-advisor.js`**:
   - `createRoadAheadProvider({ spots })`: Filters crowd-sourced hazards within lookahead distance and ±30° of heading, sorted nearest first. Designed as an interface to accept future Mappls map data.
   - `trackCurvature(fixes)`: Evaluates road curvature from the last 20 seconds of GPS fixes (`degPer100m`), flagging `inBend` if ≥ 15°/100m.
   - `adviseOvertake({ position, speedKmh, provider, fixes, now, lang })`: Pure decision engine returning `avoid`, `caution`, or `no_known_hazard` with reason codes and localized spoken phrases in English, Hindi, and Telugu.
   - `createOvertakeWatcher({ clock, lang })`: State machine for unprompted warnings strictly when `avoid` is caused by a bend or hazard, gated at speed ≥ 40 km/h, rate-limited to 1 per 3 minutes, quiet for 2 minutes after other speech, and capped at 6 per hour.
   - `isNight(now)`: Pure function for night checks (23:00 to 05:00).
2. **`src/lib/ride-guard.js`**:
   - Wired in `createOvertakeWatcher` and `createRoadAheadProvider` using the existing hazard spots buffer and a rolling 20-second GPS fixes buffer.
   - Checks on speed and heading updates; speaks unprompted avoid warnings when overlay is not active; emits `onState('overtake', alert)`.
3. **`tests/overtake-advisor.test.mjs`**:
   - Comprehensive unit test suite covering constants, curvature bend mathematics, heading filters, night logic, each advice branch and reason, rate limits, and safety regex checks.
4. **`docs/overtake-advisor-v1.md`**:
   - Full specification of known data, unknown data, threshold rationale, decision flow, multilingual phrasing, and handoff note for the Intent Layer thread.

### Safety Limits & Wording
- The application **never claims the road is clear**, **never says "safe"**, and **never says "you can overtake"**.
- All advice phrases explicitly instruct the driver to check their mirrors and oncoming traffic.
- Report-only and advisory: does not trigger automated calls, SMS, or SOS.
