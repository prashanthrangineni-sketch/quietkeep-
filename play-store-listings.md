## Banned-terms gate

Search the title, short description, full description, developer name, icon text,
screenshot text and feature graphic of **every locale** for these. Any hit is a
rejection, and at this point a rejection is an account risk.

**Ranking / superlative claims — the ones that got us rejected:**
`first` · `India's first` · `#1` · `No.1` · `No 1` · `best` · `top` · `leading`
· `award` · `award-winning` · `App of the Year` · `most popular` · `fastest`
· `only app` · `world-class` · `unbeatable` · `voted`

**Store-programme / promotional claims:**
`Editor's Choice` · `Google Play featured` · `trending` · `bestseller`
· `sale` · `discount` · `free download` or any price in the **title or icon**
(prices inside the description body are fine)

**Also disallowed by the Metadata policy:**
- Unattributed or anonymous user testimonials
- Repetitive or unrelated keyword stuffing
- Emoji or text in the icon indicating price or promotion

One-line check against a local clone:

```bash
grep -rniE "india's first|\bfirst\b|#1|no\.? ?1|\bbest\b|\btop\b|leading|award|app of the year|most popular|fastest|only app|editor'?s choice" play-store-listings.md
```
